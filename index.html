A Roof Made for Life on the St. Johns River

Palatka sits on a wide bend of the St. Johns River, and that setting shapes what a roof has to handle: humid river air, long summer afternoons of thunderstorms, and the occasional tropical system tracking up the peninsula. Around town you'll find everything from historic homes near the riverfront and the downtown district to ranch houses, manufactured homes and rural properties out toward East Palatka, Interlachen, San Mateo and Crescent City. Many of those homes are on their second or third shingle roof.

Metal roofing is a popular upgrade here because they shed heavy rain quickly, resist the mildew and algae that streak shingles in damp river climates, and reflect summer heat. Palatka Storm, Climate & Permit Notes

Storms: Putnam County sees frequent summer lightning and downpours, and tropical systems have brought high winds and St. Johns River flooding to the area in recent hurricane seasons. Properly fastened metal roofs are designed to resist wind uplift and shed water fast.

Wind & code: Roofs in Florida must meet the Florida Building Code for wind loads. Your installer will choose the roofing profile, fastening pattern and underlayment to meet requirements for your exact location.

Permits: Reroofing generally requires a permit. Inside city limits that's typically through the City of Palatka; outside the city it's through Putnam County Building & Zoning. Your licensed installer pulls the permit and schedules inspections — confirm details with them before work begins.

Palatka Metal Roofing FAQ

Does Mission Metals install my roof?

No. Mission Metals Manufacturing supplies the metal roofing, trim and accessories. We connect Palatka homeowners with independent, licensed installers who use our materials and contract directly with you.

Who issues roofing permits in Palatka and Putnam County?

Properties inside Palatka city limits go through the City of Palatka's building department; addresses in unincorporated Putnam County, such as East Palatka, Interlachen or San Mateo, go through the Putnam County Building Department. Your installer confirms which office applies and pulls the permit.

How does metal roofing handle humidity along the St. Johns River?

Damp, shaded lots near the river and its creeks are tough on shingles, which hold moisture and grow algae streaks. Coated metal roofing sheds water and dries faster; your installer can recommend underlayment and ventilation suited to a humid riverfront home.

Is metal roofing a good fit for older homes near downtown Palatka?

Often, yes. 5V crimp has a traditional Old Florida look that suits many older homes and cottages. If your home is in a designated historic district, ask your installer to check any local review requirements before work is scheduled.

What wind requirements apply to a Putnam County roof?

All re-roofs in Florida follow the Florida Building Code, which sets wind-resistance and fastening requirements based on the site's design wind speed and exposure. Open lake and river frontage can mean higher exposure, so your installer specifies fasteners and spacing for your exact address.

Can I put metal roofing on a mobile home or barn out near Interlachen or Satsuma?

Many rural Putnam County properties use PBR on barns, pole barns and sheds, and metal roofing is also common on manufactured homes. Your installer will check the structure and recommend the right profile.

How do I verify an installer is licensed?

Every installer we refer holds an active Florida roofing license. Look up any name or license number at myfloridalicense.com.

Which areas around Palatka do you cover?

All of Putnam County, including East Palatka, Interlachen, San Mateo, Crescent City, Satsuma, Welaka and Pomona Park.
